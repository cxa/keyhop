## 260904.0

KeyHop’s first release makes everyday app switching immediate and predictable.

- Double-tap your chosen modifier, then press a home-row key to jump directly to an assigned app.
- Assign up to ten apps and keep their positions consistent across supported keyboard layouts.
- Follow a guided first-run experience with clearer Accessibility permission recovery.
- Use the new app icon and pixel-sharp menu bar symbol, with matching balanced spacing.
- Pause KeyHop, resume it, or launch it automatically when you sign in.
